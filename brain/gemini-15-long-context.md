# Gemini 1.5: 10M Token Long-Context Multimodal Models

**Paper:** arXiv:2403.05530  
**Authors:** Google DeepMind  
**License:** Proprietary

## Overview
Next-generation multimodal models achieving near-perfect recall on up to 10M tokens — a 50x leap over competitors (Claude 3.0: 200k, GPT-4 Turbo: 128k).

## Key Insights
- **10M Token Context:** Achieves >99% retrieval accuracy up to 10M tokens; generational leap over Claude 3.0 (200k) and GPT-4 Turbo (128k)
- **Multimodal Excellence:** Processes long documents, hours of video, and audio with near-perfect fine-grained recall
- **Dual Variants:** Gemini 1.5 Pro (updated flagship) and Gemini 1.5 Flash (lightweight, efficient, minimal quality trade-off)
- **Real-World Impact:** 26-75% time savings across 10 professional job categories; learned Kalamang translation from grammar manual alone

## Technical Highlights
- Compute-efficient design with minimal regression in quality
- State-of-the-art in long-document QA, long-video QA, and long-context ASR
- Surprising emergent capability: learning rare languages from grammar manuals

## Takeaway
Gemini 1.5's breakthrough long-context window enables unprecedented document understanding and multimodal reasoning, opening new possibilities for AI assistants.
